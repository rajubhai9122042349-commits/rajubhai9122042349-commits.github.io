ABOUT GYRO DODGE

A challenging gyroscope-controlled arcade experience.

Gyro Dodge is a mobile arcade game where your device movement becomes your controller. Guide your ball through incoming obstacles and carefully navigate through gaps.


Stay focused, improve your reflexes, achieve higher scores and challenge yourself to survive longer.
